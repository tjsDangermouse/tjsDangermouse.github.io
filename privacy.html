Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Orbits does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. Orbits has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Network access

Orbits makes no network requests. The game, its artwork, and its typeface are bundled inside the app and run entirely on your device, including in Airplane Mode. Your daily puzzle is calculated on your device from the date, not downloaded.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, in its own storage, so your game continues where you left off:

  • your progress on the current day's puzzle
  • your win/loss statistics and streak
  • your preferences (background rotation, planet display mode)
  • whether you have completed the onboarding

This information never leaves your device. It is not backed up to us, because there is no “us” to back it up to. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Children

Orbits is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
